Agent Payments Protocol · AP2 v0.2

Can AI agents pay at your shop?

Enter your domain. We read your public UCP profile and A2A agent card, check your AP2 declaration and signing keys against the published specs and, if you paste one checkout response, verify your merchant signature.

Verify your checkout signature (optional)

Paste one checkout response from your test checkout: a UCP checkout JSON with ap2.merchant_authorization, or an AP2 checkout_jwt. It is checked in memory and never stored. Do not paste real customer data.

Read-only: we send at most three GET requests to your domain (robots.txt, /.well-known/ucp, /.well-known/agent-card.json) and never call your checkout.

Based on AP2 v0.2, UCP 2026-08-25 AP2 mandates and A2A 1.0. AP2 is a pre-release protocol under standardisation at the FIDO Alliance.
